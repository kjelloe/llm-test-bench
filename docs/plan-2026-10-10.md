## Decision rules
- The split is the cause if the single-GPU arm has no hang in 51 answers while the split arm keeps hanging.
- A config counts as fixing the hang at zero hangs in 12 runs of the reproducer (tonight's rate was 1 in 4
  for deepseek-r1:32b, 1 in 9 long answers for gpt-oss:120b), and it must not lose a task the model passes.
- A speed fix counts at more than 1.5× on 4,000-token answers.
- If only the master build helps, gpt-oss:120b and the split models get their own `LLAMA_SERVER_BIN`; the
  `67a17c17c` pin stays for qwen3.8-flash-next.
